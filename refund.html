Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

When you can get your money back for online card payments, and how to ask.

1. Failed or duplicate charges

If your card was charged but your balance or time was not added, or you were charged twice for the same purchase, we refund the amount to the same card within [[TO FILL: N]] working days after we confirm the problem. Your bank may need extra days to show it.

Staff can also fix a missing balance or time directly at the lounge; just give them the payment reference shown in the portal.

2. Unused balance

Refund policy for unused balance: [[TO FILL: unused balance refund policy]].

Balance that was already used for play time, food or drinks is not refundable.

3. Time packages

Refund policy for time packages: [[TO FILL: time package refund policy]].

Time that was already used, or that has expired, is not refundable (see the validity on each package).

4. Cancellation

Online purchases are applied to your account immediately, so they cannot be cancelled once the payment succeeds, except as described above. Cancellation window, if any: [[TO FILL: cancellation window]].

A payment that fails or that you cancel on the card page does not charge you.

Room bookings made through WhatsApp: [[TO FILL: booking cancellation notice]].

5. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original card through Paymob. We do not refund card payments in cash. The time it takes after we send it depends on your bank.

6. How to ask for a refund

  • Contact us from the Contact page, by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • Tell us your username, the date and time, the amount in EGP and the payment reference.
  • Never send your full card number or security code. We do not need them.

We will answer within [[TO FILL: response time]].

7. Prices

All amounts are in Egyptian pounds (EGP). See the Terms & Conditions for prices and VAT.
