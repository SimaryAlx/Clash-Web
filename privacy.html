Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

What personal data Clash Esports keeps, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is [[TO FILL: legal company name]], running Clash Esports (Zayed and October branches). Contact: see the Contact page.

2. What we keep

  • Your name and lounge username.
  • Your phone number and/or email address.
  • Your purchase history: top-ups, time packages, amounts in EGP, dates and payment status.
  • Your usage at the lounge (sessions, time used and balance), which our lounge management system records so the service works.

We do not keep your card number, expiry date or security code. Card details go straight to Paymob, our payment gateway; we only receive the result of the payment (paid or not), the amount and a reference.

3. Why we keep it

  • To run your account and log you in.
  • To add the balance or time you paid for and show your history.
  • To find and fix payment problems, and to handle refunds.
  • To keep the lounge safe and fair, and to meet legal and tax duties.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising. We share only what is needed with the services that help us run the platform: Paymob (payments), our lounge management system, and our hosting and database providers. We also share data if an Egyptian authority legally requires it.

5. Cookies and local storage

The portal keeps a login token and your language choice in your browser (local storage) so you stay logged in and see the site in your language. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can clear this any time in your browser settings, or by logging out.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account and purchase records while your account is active and for as long as accounting and tax rules require: [[TO FILL: data retention period]].

7. Your rights

In line with the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020), you may ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Write to us on the Contact page with your username. We may keep what the law requires us to keep, such as invoices.

8. Security

We protect data with access controls and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

9. Children

Players under [[TO FILL: minimum age]] should register with a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

2. إيه اللي بنحتفظ بيه

  • اسمك واسم المستخدم بتاعك في الصالة.
  • رقم تليفونك و/أو الإيميل.
  • سجل مشترياتك: الشحن والباقات والمبالغ بالجنيه والتواريخ وحالة الدفع.

  • عشان نضيف الرصيد أو الوقت اللي دفعت فيه ونعرض سجلك.
  • عشان نكتشف مشاكل الدفع ونحلها ونتعامل مع الاسترجاع.
  • عشان نحافظ على أمان الصالة وعدالتها ونلتزم بالواجبات القانونية والضريبية.

4. بنشاركها مع مين

البوابة بتحفظ مفتاح الدخول واللغة اللي اخترتها في متصفحك (التخزين المحلي) عشان تفضل داخل وتشوف الموقع بلغتك. مبنستخدمش كوكيز إعلانات أو تتبّع. تقدر تمسحهم في أي وقت من إعدادات المتصفح أو بتسجيل الخروج.
